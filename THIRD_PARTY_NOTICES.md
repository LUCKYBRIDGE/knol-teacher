# Third-Party Components

Current package references include CommunityToolkit.Mvvm, DirectShowLib.NET, Microsoft.Extensions.DependencyInjection, Microsoft.Extensions.Hosting, Microsoft.Web.WebView2, MiniExcel, NAudio, OpenCvSharp4 and its Windows/WPF packages, QRCoder, System.Speech, and WPF-UI.
